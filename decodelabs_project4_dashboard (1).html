Only 19% of orders reach Delivered, while 41% end Cancelled or Returned

So what: cancellations and returns (497 orders) outnumber deliveries (231) by more than two to one. This is the biggest operational issue in the data.

Groups: Problem = Cancelled + Returned, In progress = Pending + Shipped. Source: Project 3, query 7.

Coupons do not change basket size: every group averages within $35 of the others

So what: average order value ranges from $1,036 to $1,070, a spread of only 3.3%. The four coupon groups are also about the same size (286 to 313 orders), so these numbers give no sign that any coupon lifts or shrinks orders.

Average order value in US dollars, axis starts at $0. Highlighted column is the No Coupon baseline. Source: Project 3, query 8.

Keep the product mix, back the top channels, and fix order completion first

Situation

1,200 orders brought in $1.26M across seven products and five channels. Revenue per product sits between 12% and 15.5%.

Complication

Only 231 orders (19%) are Delivered. 497 (41%) are Cancelled or Returned, and coupons show no effect on basket size.

Resolution

  • Break down cancellations and returns by product and payment method.
  • Prioritise Instagram and Email, the two strongest channels.
  • Test coupons for conversion before deepening discounts.

Next step: find out why 4 in 10 orders fail, because that is where the most revenue is at stake.
